Keep your iPhone chats on your own computer

Shoebox backs up your iPhone to this computer and lets you read your chats here. Nothing is installed on your iPhone, and nothing leaves this computer unless you turn on Google Drive sync.

What you need
A USB cable, for the first connection only
Free disk space about equal to what is used on your iPhone. The backup copies the whole phone, not just chats.
Apple device drivers: found (installed with the Apple Devices app)

Connect your iPhone with a cable

Unlock your iPhone and plug it into this computer. The first connection has to be by cable. After that, backups can run over Wi-Fi.

Set a backup password

The backup is encrypted so nobody else using this computer can read your chats. Choose a password you will not forget.

{{pwHint}}
The password is saved in {{credStore}} so daily backups can run without asking you. If it is lost, existing backups cannot be opened and Apple cannot recover it.

Back up over Wi-Fi from now on

With Wi-Fi syncing on, you will not need the cable again for daily backups.
